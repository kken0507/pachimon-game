    Card Effects

    A list of every card's effect (auto-generated from the engine's effect texts). See the other chapters for class strength and the basics of summoning and battle.

    Monster Effects

    J — Signal of Revolution

    [Monster effect / once per turn] Send 1 material to the graveyard: the game enters "Revolution" until the end of this turn's End Phase (toggle).

    Q — Spell Jack

    [Monster effect / once per turn] When a magic/trap is activated, send 1 material to the graveyard to chain: negate that magic/trap and stack it under this monster as a material.

    K — King's Majesty

    [Monster effect / once per turn] Before battle resolution, send 1 material to the graveyard: this monster gains +3 attack for that battle.

    ACE — Ace Draw

    [Monster effect / once per turn] During your Main Phase, send 1 material to the graveyard: draw 1 card.

    2 — Freeze

    [Monster effect / once per turn] During your Main Phase, send 1 material to the graveyard: 1 opponent monster becomes Uncharged for 1 turn.

    Magic Cards

    3 — Reanimate

    [Magic] Choose 1 card from the graveyard and special summon it to your field as a monster.

    5 — Hand Sort

    [Magic] Cost: send 3 cards from your hand to the graveyard. Look at your opponent's hand, leave 1 card, and send the rest to the graveyard.

    7 — Hand Catch (Magic)

    [Magic] Choose 1 card from your opponent's hand and add it to your hand.

    9 — Blast

    [Magic] Target 1 opponent monster and destroy it.

    10 — Hand Crush (Magic)

    [Magic] Choose 1 card from your opponent's hand and send it to their graveyard.

    Trap Cards

    8 — Battle Break (Trap)

    [Trap] End your opponent's Battle Phase (cannot be activated the turn it was set; can only be activated during your opponent's Battle Phase).

    8 — Blocker Ability

    [Activated ability] When your opponent declares an attack, send 1 of this monster's materials to the graveyard: negate that attack (Four Card Blocker only).

    Shield Reactions

    3 — Shield Rebirth

    [Shield Reaction / mandatory] Choose 1 card from your graveyard and turn it into a shield (fizzles if your graveyard is empty).

    4 — Emergency Deploy

    [Shield Reaction / mandatory] This card becomes a monster (special summoned Charged; fizzles to the graveyard if your monster zone is full).

    6 — Shield Counterattack

    [Shield Reaction / mandatory] Destroy 1 of your opponent's shields (the shield owner chooses). The destroyed shield's trigger also chains (fizzles if the opponent has no shields).

    7 — Hand Catch

    [Shield Reaction / mandatory] Choose 1 card from your opponent's hand and add it to your hand (fizzles if their hand is empty).

    8 — Battle Break

    [Shield Reaction / mandatory] End your opponent's Battle Phase (Main Phase 2 still occurs).

    9 — Counter Blast

    [Shield Reaction / mandatory] Target and destroy 1 opponent monster (its materials also go to the graveyard; fizzles if the opponent has no monsters).

    10 — Hand Crush

    [Shield Reaction / mandatory] Choose 1 card from your opponent's hand and send it to their graveyard (fizzles if their hand is empty).

    Hand Triggers

    2 — Emergency Freeze

    [Hand trigger] When your opponent declares an attack, send this card to the graveyard: negate the attack and make the attacking monster Uncharged for 1 turn.

    Staircases

    3段 — Three-Step Counter

    [Stair] When your opponent declares an attack, send 3 consecutive classes from your hand to the graveyard: negate the attack and make the attacker Uncharged for 1 turn (2 and 3 are not consecutive).

    4段 — Four-Step Counter

    [Stair] When your opponent declares an attack, send 4 consecutive classes from your hand to the graveyard: negate the attack and destroy the attacking monster (2 and 3 are not consecutive).

    5段 — Five-Step Counter

    [Stair] When your opponent declares an attack, send 5 consecutive classes from your hand to the graveyard: negate the attack, make the attacker Uncharged for 1 turn, then special summon 1 card from the graveyard as a monster (2 and 3 are not consecutive).

    Four Cards

    3 — Double Reanimate

    [Four Card] Choose 1 card from each graveyard (yours and your opponent's) and special summon them to your field as monsters (requires 2+ empty monster zone slots).

    4 — Victory Fanfare

    [Four Card] You win the game.

    5 — Deck Operation

    [Four Card] Each player picks 1 card from the deck; yours goes on top, theirs goes on the bottom (the activating player chooses first).

    6 — Shield Burst

    [Four Card] Destroy 2 of your shields to destroy 3 of your opponent's shields (requires you to have 2+ and the opponent 3+ shields; all triggers activate).

    7 — Lucky Seven

    [Four Card] Toss a coin. Heads: add your opponent's entire hand to yours. Tails: your opponent draws 2 cards.

    8 — Four Card Blocker

    [Four Card] Special summon 1 of the 4 cards as a monster with the other 3 as materials (the 4 cards do not go to the graveyard). Attack = 6 + number of materials (9 with 3). When your opponent declares an attack, you may send 1 material to the graveyard to negate it (with 0 materials the ability is unusable and attack is 6).

    9 — Magic Burst

    [Four Card] Destroy all cards in your opponent's magic & trap zone.

    10 — Metal Pot

    [Four Card] Both players discard their entire hand. Then, starting with you, each player draws 5 cards from the deck.

    J — Revolution

    [Four Card] Start a revolution — the lower attack power wins battles (toggle; ends in the End Phase).

    Q — Mind Control

    [Four Card] Take control of 1 opponent monster until the End Phase (requires an empty monster zone slot; it goes to the graveyard if there is no room to return).

    K — King's Blessing

    [Four Card] 1 monster on the field gains +3 attack (permanent; lost when destroyed).

    ACE — Ace Search

    [Four Card] Choose 1 card from the deck and add it to your hand.

    2 — Great Freeze

    [Four Card] All of your opponent's monsters become Uncharged for 2 turns (the count decreases during the opponent's Standby Phase).
