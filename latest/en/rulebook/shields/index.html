    Shields

    Shields are face-down defensive cards. Each player starts with 5, and their contents are hidden.

    Shield destruction sequence

    1. Select: the attacker chooses which shield to destroy (the defender cannot choose).
    2. Reveal: flip the chosen shield face up.
    3. Trigger check: if the card has a trigger effect, it activates mandatorily.
    4. Destination: by default to the graveyard, though some effects send it elsewhere (below).

    If the activation condition cannot be met, the effect fizzles and the card is sent to the graveyard.

    Classes with trigger effects

    Class Trigger effect Destination after destruction
    3 Turn one card from your graveyard into a shield Returned to the deck and shuffled
    4 Becomes a monster (fizzles if the zone is full) Field (special-summoned Charged) / graveyard if full
    6 Destroy one of the opponent's shields (the holder chooses) Graveyard
    7 Take one card from the opponent's hand Graveyard
    8 End the opponent's Battle phase (Main phase 2 still occurs) Graveyard
    9 Destroy one of the opponent's monsters Graveyard
    10 Send one card from the opponent's hand to their graveyard Graveyard

    Classes 5, J, Q, K, ACE, and 2 have no trigger effect and go to the graveyard when destroyed.

    Chains

    The class 6 trigger causes another shield to be destroyed, so the destroyed shield's trigger can activate in a chain.

    Zero shields

    Having 0 shields is not an immediate loss. You lose only when a direct attack succeeds.
